Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026 | Last Updated: March 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Athlete Privacy

Solxce ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal and athletic data. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use the Solxce iOS application and associated services, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your legal rights regarding your data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health, workout, camera, or nutrition records to third-party data brokers or advertising platforms.

2. Information We Collect

  • Athlete Profile Data: Name, handle, bio, athletic archetype, and optional avatar image.
  • Training & Workout Data: Sets, reps, weights, exercise selections, volume metrics, and personal records.
  • Run & Outdoor Activity Data: Distance, pace, splits, cadence, and GPS route coordinates when you track runs.
  • Nutrition & Fasting Data: Food logs, estimated calories, macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat), water intake, and fasting target windows.
  • Camera & Image Data: Photos captured for AI-assisted food recognition and macro estimation. Photos are processed strictly to recognize food and are not used for facial recognition or external marketing.
  • Apple HealthKit Integration: With your permission, Solxce reads and writes workout sessions, active energy, heart rate, and sleep data. HealthKit data is never used for advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide and enhance your athlete experience:

  • Personalizing AI workout recommendations and recovery insights.
  • Syncing your records securely across your iPhone, Apple Watch, and cloud backups.
  • Displaying athlete community achievements if you explicitly choose to share posts.
  • Managing subscriptions, in-app purchases, and customer support.

4. Apple HealthKit Data Protection

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, Solxce guarantees that data obtained through the HealthKit framework is never shared with third parties for advertising or data mining. You can toggle or revoke HealthKit permissions at any time via iOS Settings.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your fitness data. You can delete individual logs or request complete permanent account deletion directly inside the app under Settings > Reset Account or by contacting us at support@solxce.app.
